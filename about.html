Why The Inner Atlas?

The Inner Atlas is a personal space for exploring ideas and making sense of the world. It brings together notes, reflections and things worth learning about.

Here, you may find writing about philosophy, science, psychology, technology, books and everyday observations. The subjects may change; the curiosity stays.

This is a work in progress. The best way to shape it is to keep reading, questioning and writing.
